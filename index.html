02 / THE RESEARCH QUESTIONFROM TREATMENT TO TRADE-OFFS

Safety profiles diverge
across therapeutic classes.

Resistant hypertension is generally defined as blood pressure that remains uncontrolled despite three appropriate agents including a diuretic, or control that requires four or more agents[12,13].

This project compares the reported adverse-event patterns of newer non-steroidal MRA, ARNI, and dual ERA therapies against conventional steroidal MRAs. The analysis examines how electrolyte, endocrine, hemodynamic, and fluid-related reporting signals diverge across these classes.

Clinical context: this FAERS analysis does not establish that every included report meets the clinical definition of resistant hypertension.

01 / ELECTROLYTES↘

31.8%

Lower reporting odds than spironolactone; a global signal remains.

Finerenone versus spironolactone for hyperkalemia: head-to-head ROR 0.68 (95% CI 0.50-0.93). A global finerenone hyperkalemia signal remains.

02 / ENDOCRINE↗

10.85 ROR

Disproportionate endocrine reporting with spironolactone.

Spironolactone endocrine reporting was disproportionate (ROR 10.85; 95% CI 5.75-20.48). Finerenone's estimate was 1.03 (0.54-1.99), with 12 endocrine reports recorded, which does not equal zero events. The antiandrogenic pharmacology of spironolactone is documented in the endocrine literature[26].

ABSTRACT · GLOBAL BACKGROUND
03 / HEMODYNAMICS↗

6.06 ROR

Hypotension and angioedema signals with sacubitril/valsartan.

Sacubitril/valsartan showed hypotension-related disproportionality (ROR 6.06; 95% CI 5.06-7.24), alongside an angioedema signal. Hypotension was the consistent tolerability finding in the pivotal heart failure trial[30]. The pressure endpoint includes broader postural symptoms.

ABSTRACT · GLOBAL BACKGROUND
!
Interpretation.

Reporting odds describe patterns within spontaneous reports; they do not estimate the probability that a treated patient will experience an adverse event[6,11].

Standardized terminology

RxNorm drug synonyms and MedDRA v27.0 event terms harmonize product names and clinical endpoints[3,4]. Drug roles are restricted to primary or secondary suspects. Report deduplication and name normalization are recognized prerequisites for valid FAERS analysis[10].

±

Complementary disproportionality measures

ROR with 95% confidence intervals, PRR, Bayesian IC025, and Benjamini-Hochberg FDR assess disproportionality[5,6,7,8]. The reported FDR threshold is q < 0.05.

◷

Time-to-onset modeling

Valid therapy and event dates yield onset intervals from 0-365 days. The manuscript uses 2,000 bootstrap resamples and two-parameter Weibull fits[9].

Interpretation of the
Weibull shape parameter.

The fitted Weibull shape for finerenone hyperkalemia is 1.432. Within the dated reports, it describes an increasing modeled hazard with elapsed time[9]. It does not estimate the hazard for all treated patients.

Limitations and

ROR 9.00 (95% CI 3.31-24.46). A wide interval and sparse data require hypothesis-generating interpretation, regardless of the reported q-value. No post-marketing pharmacovigilance study of aprocitentan has been published to date; this analysis is among the first reported assessments of its spontaneous-report safety profile.

01No exposure denominator +

FAERS does not contain the total number of patients who took each drug. Report percentages are not adverse-event incidence, and ROR is not relative clinical risk[6,11].

02Association is not causation +

Reporting disproportionality can flag a pattern, but cannot prove a drug caused an event. Mechanistic explanations are plausibility arguments, not causal findings[11].

03Different patients, different indications +

Chronic kidney disease, heart failure, concomitant therapies, and prescribing selection can influence both drug exposure and reported events. The analysis does not confirm a strictly resistant-hypertension cohort.

04Missingness and reporting bias +

Underreporting, stimulated reporting, duplicate submissions, and incomplete dates affect interpretation. For example, only 57 of 291 finerenone hyperkalemia reports contribute to that onset analysis. Reporting behavior, including geographic and temporal clustering, can drive disproportionality independent of pharmacology[10].

05No treatment recommendation +

This site explains a research project. It does not recommend starting, replacing, or stopping therapy. Patient-specific decisions require clinical assessment and appropriate guidelines[13,14].

Source consistency & provenance notes +

Displayed values are reported estimates. This atlas transcribes the supplied abstract and manuscript; it does not independently authenticate the raw FDA records or certify the analytical pipeline. External citations support interpretation; they do not validate the FAERS arithmetic.

    The abstract is a supplied conference-submission document, not verification of publication or peer review. Its causal language has been softened here to reflect the limitations of spontaneous-reporting evidence.
