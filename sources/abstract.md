### **ABSTRACT BODY**

**Objectives:** Novel non-steroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists (Finerenone), angiotensin receptor-neprilysin inhibitors (Sacubitril/Valsartan), and dual endothelin receptor antagonists (Aprocitentan; FDA-approved March 2024) offer emerging pathways in resistant hypertension. However, comparative real-world post-marketing safety regarding hyperkalemia, antiandrogenic endocrine disruption, and hemodynamic vulnerability remains poorly defined. We evaluated genuine pharmacovigilance signals across 6,601 deduplicated FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS) records.

**Methods:** We ingested 7,519 post-marketing reports from the US FDA openFDA API, applying longitudinal deduplication (CaseID versioning and demographic-event matching). Disproportionality was quantified via Reporting Odds Ratios (ROR, 95% CI), Proportional Reporting Ratios (PRR), and Bayesian Information Components (IC025), adjusted with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR (q < 0.05). Time-to-onset (TTO; Bootstrap 95% CI) and two-parameter Weibull failure modeling were evaluated. Aprocitentan analysis represents a 100% exhaustive census of available FAERS records (n = 17 unique cases; exploratory/sentinel observations).

**Results:** Among 6,601 unique patients, Spironolactone generated marked endocrine/antiandrogenic signals (ROR 10.85, 95% CI 5.75–20.48, IC025 +2.08, q < 0.001) and prominent hyperkalemia (ROR 4.53, 95% CI 3.35–6.12). Conversely, Finerenone exhibited no endocrine signals (ROR 1.03, 95% CI 0.54–1.99, q = 1.000) and demonstrated 31.8% lower odds of hyperkalemia versus Spironolactone (ROR 0.68, 95% CI 0.50–0.93, p = 0.017). Sacubitril/Valsartan displayed orthostatic hypotension disproportionality (ROR 6.06, 95% CI 5.06–7.24, q < 0.001) and angioedema (ROR 3.33, 95% CI 2.05–5.40). Sentinel surveillance of Aprocitentan (n = 17 total, n = 6 events; exploratory) identified peripheral edema (ROR 9.00, 95% CI 3.31–24.46, IC025 +1.34, q < 0.001; wide CI reflects small cohort) without electrolyte disturbances. Median hyperkalemia onset was 95.0 days (IQR 55.0–148.0, Weibull beta 1.43).

**Conclusion:** In real-world practice, Finerenone mitigates hyperkalemia and eliminates antiandrogenic toxicities inherent to Spironolactone. Dual neurohormonal and endothelin agents introduce divergent liabilities, including hypotension and fluid retention, mandating individualized organ-specific prescribing in resistant hypertension.

**Keywords:** Resistant Hypertension; Finerenone; Mineralocorticoid Receptor Antagonists; Pharmacovigilance; Hyperkalemia and Endocrine Disruption.
